Our story

One specialist agency.
Two decades of growth.

SCENSOB was founded in Birmingham in 2008 by Claire Hendricks, a former logistics manager who believed the transport sector deserved a staffing partner that actually understood operations — not just CVs.

The early years were focused entirely on vehicle hire and courier delivery. Building compliance into every placement before it was required. Verifying licences before sending drivers to sites. That rigour became our reputation.

In 2012, three NHS supply chain clients asked whether we could source GDP-compliant pharmaceutical drivers. We said yes, built the protocol properly, and the pharmaceutical logistics practice became one of our strongest specialisms.

By 2016 we had offices in London, Manchester, and Bristol. By 2019, the SCENSOB Compliance Framework had been formalised across all placements.

In 2020, long-standing clients began asking whether the same compliance culture could be applied to technology roles. SCENSOB IT was launched. In 2024, we formally became SCENSOB Group — one organisation, two divisions, the same standard.

What we stand for

Our values

Sector depth, not breadth

We operate in two fields and know them with genuine expertise. We don’t try to be everything to everyone — we’d rather be the best at what we do.

Compliance as standard

Every placement includes the verification, briefing, and documentation relevant to its sector. Compliance is never an add-on or an upgrade — it’s built in.

Operational understanding

Our consultants have worked in or adjacent to the industries they serve. That means faster shortlists, better brief interpretation, and fewer surprises.

Guaranteed delivery

SLA commitments across both divisions. If a placement doesn’t work out within 48 hours, we replace at no cost. No arguments, no exceptions.
