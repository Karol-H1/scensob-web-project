About the Group

Built on expertise,
expanded by demand.

SCENSOB was founded in 2008 as a specialist transport staffing agency in Birmingham. Over 18 years, we built deep expertise in vehicle hire, courier delivery, and pharmaceutical logistics — and a reputation for compliance no other agency could match.

In 2020, clients began asking for the same rigour applied to technology placements. SCENSOB IT was born. Today the Group serves businesses across both sectors from four UK offices.
