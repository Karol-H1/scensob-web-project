About SCENSOB IT

Technology expertise.
Compliance built in.

SCENSOB IT is the technology division of SCENSOB Group, bringing the same compliance-first culture to IT staffing, managed services, and digital projects.

Our story

Born from client demand.
Built to the same standard.

By 2019, SCENSOB’s transport staffing reputation had spread beyond logistics. Long-standing clients were asking a recurring question: “Can you apply the same rigour to our IT hires?”

The answer was yes — but only if we built it properly. In 2020, SCENSOB IT launched with a small team of specialist IT recruiters and an infrastructure engineer. The same compliance framework that underpinned transport placements was adapted for technology roles: technical assessments, reference verification, IR35 support, and sector-specific onboarding.

Managed services followed organically. Clients who trusted us for staffing began asking us to manage their IT operations. A cloud practice launched in 2021. Cybersecurity in 2022. Software development in 2023.

Today, SCENSOB IT serves 50+ managed clients across professional services, financial services, logistics, healthcare, and technology sectors — with the full backing of the SCENSOB Group infrastructure.
