About SCENSOB

Built by transport people, for transport people.

SCENSOB was founded in 2011 by a logistics manager who was tired of agencies that didn't understand the difference between a Class 1 and a Class 2 — let alone GDP. Fifteen years later, we're still the agency that gets it.

We started with one sector — vehicle and equipment hire — because we knew it well. As our clients grew, they asked us to help with their courier networks. Then a cold-chain operator asked if we could source GDP-aware pharmaceutical drivers. We said yes, built the protocol, and made it our standard.

Today, SCENSOB operates across three specialist sectors from four UK offices. Every placement we make goes through the same rigorous compliance screening — whether it's a hire desk coordinator in Coventry or a pharmaceutical courier running a controlled drugs route into London.

The agency was built on a simple belief: in transport and logistics, the cost of a bad hire isn't just financial. It's reputational, regulatory, and sometimes physical. So we don't cut corners. And we never will.

How we work

Sector depth over breadth

We work in three sectors and we know them inside out. We don't try to be everything to everyone.

Compliance as standard

Licence checks, GDP briefings, DBS screening — these aren't add-ons. They're in every placement, every time.

Candidates are clients too

We treat drivers and operatives with the same care as the companies we place them with. Good candidates come back.

Built for the UK

We operate across England from four regional offices. Local knowledge, national reach.
